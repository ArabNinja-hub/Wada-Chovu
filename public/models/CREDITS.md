# 3D model credits

These are placeholder product models from the Khronos glTF Sample Models repository
(https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Models, folders 2.0/WaterBottle, 2.0/Avocado and
2.0/BoxTextured). Each was resized to 512 px textures with `@gltf-transform/cli` (`resize`) and
otherwise left unchanged.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `water-bottle.glb` | WaterBottle | CC0 1.0 (public domain) | Microsoft, waived all copyright |
| `avocado.glb` | Avocado | CC0 1.0 (public domain) | Microsoft, waived all copyright |
| `box-textured.glb` | BoxTextured | CC BY 4.0 | Khronos Group glTF sample models. Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ |

None of these is a picture of a Chovu Chovu product. Replace them with real product models
when they are available, by changing `url` on the matching `model.*` entry in `src/content/assets.ts`.
